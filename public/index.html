                  How TradeGuide works

                  The signal (−100 to +100)

                  Seven factors each vote between −1 (bearish) and +1 (bullish), weighted and averaged:

                  • Trend (25%) — price vs the 50- and 200-period averages, their alignment and slope, scaled by ADX trend strength.
                  • MACD momentum (15%) — direction and acceleration of momentum, crossovers.
                  • RSI (15%) — healthy momentum vs overbought/oversold extremes.
                  • Breakout (15%) — where price sits in its recent 20-bar range; breakouts and breakdowns.
                  • Bollinger %B (10%) — how stretched price is from its average.
                  • Stochastic (10%) — short-term turns in oversold/overbought zones.
                  • Volume / OBV (10%) — whether volume confirms the move.

                  +50 or more Strong Buy · +20 Buy · −20 to +20 Hold/Wait · −20 Sell · −50 or less Strong Sell. Confidence is how many factors agree, reduced when risk is extreme.

                  The risk score (1–10)

                  Built from annualised volatility, worst drawdown in the last year, liquidity (dollar volume), asset class (crypto adds risk), penny-stock status and beta. Key terms:

                  • Volatility — how much price typically swings in a year. 20% is calm for a stock; 80%+ is normal for many coins.
                  • Max drawdown — the biggest peak-to-trough fall in the period. Ask yourself whether you could sit through it.
                  • VaR 95% — on 1 bar in 20 you should expect to lose at least this much.
                  • Beta — sensitivity to the market (S&P 500 for stocks, Bitcoin for crypto). 1.5 means roughly 1.5× the market's moves.
                  • ATR — average true range, the typical size of one bar. Stops are set in multiples of ATR so normal noise doesn't knock you out.

                  The trade plan

                  The stop goes ~2 ATR away (or just past the recent swing low), targets at 2× and 3× the risk. Position size = account × risk % ÷ distance to stop, capped at 25% of the account. Risking 1% per trade means 10 losses in a row costs about 10% — survivable.

                  Rules that matter more than any signal

                  1. Decide your exit before you enter. Put the stop in the broker, not in your head.
                  2. Risk 0.5–2% of the account per trade, never more.
                  3. Don't buy a "Strong Buy" with Extreme risk using money you need.
                  4. Check earnings dates and news — technicals can't see them coming.
                  5. Look at the back-test card. If the signal has lost money on this chart, it's telling you something.
                  6. Paper-trade a strategy for a few weeks before using real money.
